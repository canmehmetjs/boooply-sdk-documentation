## How Credits Work

Interviews and team meetings use the same credits, but at different moments.

### Interviews: counted when you create them

Interview credits are counted when an interview is **created**, based on its scheduled length:

- **1 credit** covers an AI interview scheduled up to **30 minutes**.
- An AI interview scheduled **longer than 30 minutes** uses **2 credits**.
- **1-to-1 interviews** always use **1 credit**, whatever their length.

The cost is fixed when you create the interview, so you always know the price up front. The composer shows a "Uses 2 interview credits" hint when a longer AI interview is being set up.

### Team meetings: counted when they start

Team meetings are free without transcription. With transcript and AI recap they use 1 credit when the meeting starts, and 2 credits if 11 or more people join.

- Nothing is taken when you create or schedule a team meeting.
- The credit is taken when the **first person joins**, and only if transcription is on.
- The second credit is taken when the **11th person joins**. Each person counts once, even if they leave and rejoin. A meeting never uses more than 2 credits.
- A meeting nobody joins uses nothing.
- If you have **no credits left**, the call still works, but it is not recorded or transcribed and gets no recap. The host sees a short note in the call.
- Meetings booked through your booking page follow the same rule.

## No-Show Refunds

No-show refunds are for **interviews**. You only pay for interviews that actually happen. A credit comes back **automatically** when:

- the candidate **never joined** the interview, or
- the interview was **cancelled before it started**.

Refunded credits are added to your **add-on credit balance** (they never expire), and each refund appears in the transaction list on your billing page. There is nothing to configure and nothing to request.

Team meetings do not need refunds: a team meeting only uses a credit once it has started, and a started meeting is not refunded.

- **AI chat messages** reset daily.

## Plan Changes

### Upgrading
Upgrades take effect **immediately**. You'll be charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the billing period, and your limits increase right away.

### Downgrading
Downgrades take effect at the **end of your current billing period**. You keep your current plan's limits until then.

### Cancelling
When you cancel, your subscription remains active until the end of the billing period. After that, your account reverts to the Free plan. Your data is never deleted, but you won't be able to create new candidates or jobs beyond the free limits until you resubscribe.

### Add-on credits after plan changes
Add-on interview credits are not affected by plan changes. They remain in your account regardless of upgrades, downgrades, or cancellation.

## Auto-Recharge

You can enable auto-recharge to automatically purchase add-on interview credits when your total available interviews drop below a threshold. Configure this in your billing settings:

- **Threshold**: When to trigger (5, 10, 25, or 50 credits remaining)
- **Pack**: Which add-on pack to purchase (Small, Medium, or Large; the Event bundle is not offered for auto-recharge)

Auto-recharge uses your saved payment method and charges automatically.
