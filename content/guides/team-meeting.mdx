# Team Meetings

Internal team meetings with video recording, transcription, and AI-generated recaps.

## Create a Team Meeting

```javascript
const { meeting } = await client.interviews.create({
  type: 'team',
  scheduledAt: '2026-04-01T10:00:00Z',
  durationMinutes: 60,
  participants: [
    { name: 'Alice', email: 'alice@company.com', role: 'HOST' },
    { name: 'Bob', email: 'bob@company.com', role: 'PARTICIPANT' },
    { name: 'Carol', email: 'carol@company.com', role: 'PARTICIPANT' },
    // External participants (non-team members) work too — just provide
    // name + email. They'll join as PARTICIPANT, never as HOST.
    { name: 'Dana Ng', email: 'dana@external.io', role: 'PARTICIPANT' },
  ],
  agenda: ['Q1 review', 'Roadmap planning', 'Hiring updates'],
  transcriptionEnabled: true,
});

// Each participant gets their own join URL:
const alice = meeting.participants.find(p => p.role === 'HOST');
const aliceJoinUrl = `${meeting.url}?token=${alice.joinToken}`;
```

> **Dashboard UX:** In the Boooply dashboard you create team meetings from the **Meetings** page (see below). You can search your team, add guests by email, paste many emails at once, or pick a saved group. The SDK accepts the same flat `participants` array regardless of how the meeting was created.

### Parameters

| Field | Type | Default | Description |
|-------|------|---------|-------------|
| `participants` | `array` | Required | Must include at least one `HOST` |
| `scheduledAt` | `string` | — | ISO 8601 datetime |
| `durationMinutes` | `number` | `30` | Meeting duration |
| `agenda` | `string[]` | `[]` | Agenda items visible to all participants |
| `transcriptionEnabled` | `boolean` | `false` | Enable video recording & post-meeting transcription |

## Recording & Transcription

When `transcriptionEnabled: true`:

1. **Video recording** starts automatically when participants join (Room Composite — all participants in one video)
2. **Audio tracks** are recorded individually per participant (for accurate speaker attribution)
3. The host can toggle recording on/off during the meeting via the **REC** button

When the meeting ends:

1. Individual audio tracks are transcribed using AI speech-to-text
2. Speaker names are automatically matched to participants (no generic "Speaker 0" labels)
3. Transcripts are merged by timestamp into a unified timeline

## AI Recap

After transcription completes, the AI generates:

- **Summary** — 3-5 sentence overview of the meeting
- **Key Topics** — what was discussed and by whom
- **Action Items** — what needs to be done, who's responsible, deadlines
- **Decisions** — what was agreed upon
- **Open Questions** — unresolved issues
- **Meeting Stats** — dominant speakers, meeting tone, participation balance

All participants with email addresses receive the recap automatically.

## In-Meeting Features

During the meeting, participants have access to:

- **Shared Notes** — collaborative bullet-point notes visible to everyone
- **Agenda** — checkable agenda items (host/co-host can add, edit, check off)
- **Live Polls** — create polls, vote, see results
- **Hand Raise** — signal to speak

Shared notes, agenda state, and poll results are saved and visible on the meeting detail page after the meeting ends.

## Accessing Results

```javascript
// Get meeting details (includes recap, transcript count, video URL)
const { meeting: details } = await client.interviews.get('Team-1234567890');

// Get transcript
const { transcript } = await client.interviews.getTranscript('Team-1234567890');
// transcript: [
//   {
//     id: 'uuid-...',
//     speaker: 'Alice',
//     role: 'PARTICIPANT',
//     content: "Let's start with Q1 numbers...",
//     startMs: 1000,
//     endMs: 3500,
//     confidence: 0.94
//   },
//   ...
// ]
```

The meeting detail page shows:
- **Recap** tab — AI-generated summary, action items, decisions
- **Transcript** tab — full transcript with speaker names and timestamps
- **Agenda** tab — agenda items with completion status
- **Shared Notes** tab — collaborative notes from the meeting
- **Polls** tab — poll questions, options, and vote results
- **Notes** tab — private notes taken by participants
- **Video player** — recording with speaker timeline and talk time stats

## The Meetings Page

Team meetings have their own page in the dashboard: **Meetings** in the sidebar. The Interviews page shows interviews only.

- **Upcoming**, **Past** and **Series** tabs.
- **New meeting** opens the meeting form. You can also start one from the **Calendar** ("New meeting") or from a team chat channel.
- Meetings booked through your booking page show here too, with a **Booked** badge and their own filter. They show for their host and for owners and admins.
- Every role can create team meetings. Owners and admins see all team meetings in the workspace. Everyone else sees the meetings they organize or are invited to.

Old links to a team meeting under `/interviews/...` still work. They open the meeting on the Meetings page.

### From team chat

In a channel, use **Meet now** to start a meeting right away with everyone in the channel. The link is posted in the channel, and your own room opens in a new tab. Meet now meetings have no transcript, so they are free. Use **Schedule** to plan a meeting with the channel's people filled in. A meeting can have up to 20 people, so a bigger channel uses Schedule to pick who joins.

## Groups

A group is a saved list of people, like Engineering or Leadership. Pick a group in the meeting form to invite everyone at once.

- Owners and admins manage groups in **Settings > Team > Groups**. Everyone can use them.
- A group can have up to **20 people** (the same limit as a meeting). Add team members by search, or guests by email.
- Group names are unique in a workspace.
- Deleting a group does not change meetings that already exist. Repeating meetings that follow it keep their current people.

## Repeating Meetings

Set a meeting to repeat in the meeting form: every weekday, weekly on the days you pick, every 2 weeks, or monthly (same weekday, like "2nd Tuesday", or same date). It can end on a date, after a number of meetings, or never.

- **One invite.** Each person gets one calendar invite for the whole series (Google, Outlook, or an .ics file).
- **One link.** Each person has one personal link that always opens the right meeting, the next one or the one that is live now.
- **Edit.** Change **only this meeting** or **this and following** meetings. Cancel one meeting or the whole series.
- **Follows its group.** A series made from a group invites the group's current people. Someone removed from the group is not invited to future meetings.
- Each date is a real meeting with its own recording, transcript and recap. The series page shows them all on one timeline.

You can also ask the AI assistant, for example: "Every Tuesday at 10 with Engineering". It shows the repeat rule and the group before it creates anything.

Repeating meetings and groups are dashboard features for now. Single team meetings work through the API as before.

## Weekly Recaps and Action Items

When transcription is on, every team meeting gets an AI recap with action items. Action items are saved, so the team can follow them up.

- On a repeating meeting, open action items **carry forward** to the next meeting.
- The next recap starts with **Since last time**: what was done, what is still open, and the last decisions.
- The AI only **suggests** that an item looks done. The owner of the item or the organizer confirms it, or keeps it open.
- If nobody joins a meeting, nothing is charged, no recap is made, and open items stay open for the next one.
- On a series you can choose who gets the recap email: **everyone invited** (default), **only the organizer**, or **off**.

## Credits

Team meetings are free without transcription. With transcript and AI recap they use 1 credit when the meeting starts, and 2 credits if 11 or more people join.

- Nothing is charged when you create or schedule a team meeting. The credit is taken when the first person joins.
- If your workspace has no credits left, the call still works, but it is not recorded or transcribed. The host sees a note in the call.
