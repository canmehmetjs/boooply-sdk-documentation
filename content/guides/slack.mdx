# Slack

Ask Boooply about your own talent pool without leaving Slack.

Mention the bot in any channel with a plain sentence and it searches the people
your team has already added to Boooply, then replies with who matches and links
to their profiles.

```
@boooply find me backend engineers with Kubernetes, 5+ years, London
```

It answers with the people it found, each with an **Open in Boooply** button.

## What it searches

Only your own talent pool: candidates your workspace has already uploaded,
including their CVs, interviews and evaluations. It does not source strangers,
it does not scrape LinkedIn, and it cannot see anything outside your Boooply
account.

## Connecting it

1. Sign in to Boooply as an **owner or admin**
2. Go to **Settings → Integrations → Slack → Connect**
3. Approve the app in Slack
4. In Slack, invite the bot to a channel: `/invite @boooply`

Only owners and admins can connect or disconnect. Everyone else sees the
connection status without the buttons.

Your plan must include AI search. On a plan without it, the bot connects but
answers with an upgrade note instead of results.

## Who sees what

**Answers respect your Boooply permissions.** The bot works out who you are
from your Slack email, then answers as you:

| Your role | What the bot returns |
| --- | --- |
| Owner / Admin | The whole talent pool |
| Recruiter | Your assigned positions, or everything if you have no assignments |
| Hiring manager | Only your assigned positions |
| Interviewer | Same rule as recruiter, with fewer tools |
| No matching Boooply account | Nothing, just a note asking an admin to invite you |

That last row is deliberate. The bot never answers as a shared service account,
because that would let someone read candidates in Slack that they cannot see in
the app.

Two people asking in the same Slack thread get separate conversations, so one
person's results are never visible in another person's follow-up questions.

## What it will not do

Boooply in Slack is **read only**. It cannot create candidates, schedule
interviews, move anyone through a pipeline or message people.

Replies carry names, current roles, match scores and links. **Email addresses,
phone numbers and CV text are stripped before anything is posted**, so the
detail stays inside Boooply behind your normal access controls.

## Usage

Each question uses one AI message from your workspace's daily allowance, the
same pool the in-app assistant uses. Every reply shows what is left, for
example *"142 of 150 AI messages left today."*

## Data and privacy

Connecting Slack means candidate **names and current roles** are sent to Slack
for the replies you ask for. Slack is a US company, so this is an international
transfer. It applies only to workspaces that connect the integration; if you
never connect it, nothing about your candidates reaches Slack.

See [Privacy](https://boooply.com/privacy) for the full processor list.

## Troubleshooting

**Nothing happens when I mention it.**
The bot only sees channels it has been invited to. Run `/invite @boooply`.

**"I could not find a Boooply account for your email."**
Your Slack email has to match a member of the Boooply workspace that connected
Slack. Ask an owner or admin to invite that email address.

**I get an upgrade message instead of results.**
The workspace is on a plan without AI search. The integration connects on any
plan so you can see what it does; the answers need an upgrade.

**The answer arrived as a direct message.**
Fallback behaviour. If the bot cannot post in the channel, it sends the answer
to you directly rather than losing it.
